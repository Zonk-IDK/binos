01 — THE IDEA

Less noise.
More good stuff.

Your computer should feel like yours. BinOS aims to bring a calm, cohesive desktop experience to the flexibility of Linux — without pretending the terminal doesn't exist.

The project is in its early days. The first goal is a working Arch-based ISO with a Pantheon-inspired desktop, thoughtful defaults, and original photography.

A desktop with taste
